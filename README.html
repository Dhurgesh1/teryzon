01 / About

Understanding soil through technology.

TERYZON is an autonomous ecological survey and restoration rover designed to assess soil health and support practical environmental restoration.

The project focuses on collecting environmental measurements, analysing soil conditions and supporting strategies that can improve soil health over time.

The system is being developed with a focus on Warangal, Telangana.

02 / The Problem

Soil health needs measurable evidence.

Soil conditions can vary considerably between locations. Without regular measurements, it can be difficult to identify problems, understand their severity or determine whether restoration efforts are actually working.

TERYZON aims to make soil assessment more accessible by combining a mobile rover with environmental sensors and a local monitoring interface.

03 / Capabilities

Built for field measurement.

Soil Monitoring

Collect soil moisture, temperature, pH and electrical conductivity measurements.

Mobile Rover

A motorised platform allows the system to move between survey locations.

Probe Deployment

A servo or linear actuator can lower the sensor probe into the soil.

Local Web Interface

Monitor readings and control the rover through a local ESP32-S3 web server.

Visual Monitoring

The ESP32-CAM provides a foundation for visual observation of survey areas.

Restoration Analysis

Measurements can be compared over time to evaluate changes after restoration.

Prototype components.

09 / Impact

Measure first. Restore with evidence.

TERYZON is built around a simple principle: environmental restoration should begin with understanding the condition of the environment.

Repeated measurements can help identify changes in soil conditions and evaluate whether restoration strategies are producing measurable improvements.

The project aligns with UN Sustainable Development Goal 15 — Life on Land.
