Cookie policy.

Last updated: August 26, 2026

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser. They help websites remember preferences and understand basic usage.

How Teryzon uses them

Teryzon may use essential cookies or similar technologies to keep the website working, protect forms, remember preferences, maintain signed-in sessions, and understand aggregate performance.

Google sign-in and dashboard sessions

When Google sign-in and dashboard features are available, Teryzon and its authentication providers may use cookies or similar storage to complete sign-in, keep you signed in, prevent abuse, and protect your account. These technologies are essential to account functionality.

Third-party services

Embedded or linked services may set their own cookies when you interact with them. Their practices are governed by their own policies. Google may also use technologies as described in its own policies when you use Google sign-in.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling essential cookies may affect how account and dashboard features function.

Questions

For questions about this policy, contact us through the contact form.
