The journey behind Teryzon

From a question
to a rover.

A simple idea grew into a system for measuring what often goes unseen beneath the ground.

01

The Beginning

Why did we start?

Environmental problems are often visible above the ground, but many important changes happen underneath it. Soil can look healthy while its condition is slowly changing.

We wanted to understand what was happening beneath the surface using real measurements instead of assumptions.

02

The Question

Why did we choose soil?

Soil supports agriculture, plants, ecosystems and communities, but its health is often overlooked because many problems are difficult to see.

This led us to focus on measurable factors such as:

  • Moisture
  • Temperature
  • pH
  • Electrical conductivity
  • Humidity

The question became: If we can measure what cannot easily be seen, can we understand it better?

03

From Idea to System

Why did we make a rover?

A fixed sensor can tell us about one location, but soil conditions can change from place to place.

We wanted a system that could move, explore different areas, collect measurements and bring the data together.

That idea became TERYZON — an ecological survey and restoration rover.

04

MCOP7

Why did we make this for MCOP7?

We chose MCOP7 Target 5:

“Assess soil health in your city and develop a strategy to improve soil health.”

This gave TERYZON a clear purpose:

Measure → Understand → Develop a strategy → Test → Recheck

The rover is designed to help turn soil measurements into practical environmental decisions.

05

The Build

From concept to prototype

The idea became a physical system using:

  • ESP32-S3
  • Soil moisture sensor
  • Temperature and humidity sensors
  • pH sensor
  • OLED display
  • Motor driver and four-wheel drive
  • ESP32-CAM
  • Servo/linear actuator for probe deployment
  • Web-based rover control and monitoring

The goal was not simply to build a robot, but to create a system capable of collecting useful environmental data.

06

What Comes Next

Measure. Restore. Recheck.

The next stage is real-world testing.

TERYZON will collect measurements from different areas, analyse the results, develop improvement strategies, test those strategies, and return to measure the soil again.

Explore. Measure. Understand. Restore.

Good to know

Questions,
answered.

Curious about the rover, the data, or where Teryzon fits? Start here.

What does Teryzon measure?

The rover captures soil moisture, temperature, pH, and electrical conductivity (EC) using onboard sensors.

Where can the rover operate?

Teryzon is designed for farms, school grounds, research sites, gardens, and other land areas where soil insight matters.

How does the dashboard help?

It brings live measurements into one modern view so users can analyze conditions, identify potential problems, and understand changes over time.

Who is Teryzon for?

Farmers, students, schools, landowners, researchers, and environmental organizations can use Teryzon to make better decisions about soil.
