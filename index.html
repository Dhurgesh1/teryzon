Good to know

Questions,
answered.

Curious about the rover, the data, or where Teryzon fits? Start here.

What does Teryzon measure?

The rover captures soil moisture, temperature, pH, and electrical conductivity (EC) using onboard sensors.

Where can the rover operate?

Teryzon is designed for farms, school grounds, research sites, gardens, and other land areas where soil insight matters.

How does the dashboard help?

It brings live measurements into one modern view so users can analyze conditions, identify potential problems, and understand changes over time.

Who is Teryzon for?

Farmers, students, schools, landowners, researchers, and environmental organizations can use Teryzon to make better decisions about soil.
