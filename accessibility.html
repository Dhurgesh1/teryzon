Accessibility.

Last updated: August 26, 2026

Our goal

Teryzon aims to make its website and digital services usable by as many people as possible, including people who use assistive technology.

What we work toward

  • Clear heading structure and readable content.
  • Keyboard-friendly navigation and form controls.
  • Meaningful labels and alternative text where appropriate.
  • Responsive layouts that work across screen sizes.

Feedback

If you encounter an accessibility barrier, please tell us what happened and which page was involved through the contact form. We will review the feedback and work toward an improvement.
